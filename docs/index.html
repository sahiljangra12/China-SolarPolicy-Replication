Does China's solar policy reduce bird diversity?

The paper's numbers reproduce exactly. The evidence does not show that the policy reduced birds. It does not show that solar is harmless either.

The evidence, sorted

Eight pieces of evidence, placed by whether they support the claim that solar policy harms birds.

Supports harm

The paper's regression−0.0125, significant. Reproduced exactly.

Mixed or fragile

Dose-based DiD (dCDH)−0.03 to −0.05. Not significant with controls in fair samples; effort pre-trends fail. Solar-plant areaNegative, but vanishes once birdwatcher records are held fixed. Instrumental variableSignificant, but the instrument has a direct effect. Corrected: −0.042, not significant. Sensitivity analysisA confounder explaining 2.2% of leftover variation removes the effect. Birdwatching effortStricter policy goes with fewer records, through the urban and rural split.

No support

The same regression, fair comparisons−0.0027, p = 0.47. Staggered DiD, three estimators+0.02 to +0.08, none significant.

Project record

Everything done, in order, with its report, code and logs.

Task 1

Replication, data structure and identification

Ran the authors' code, rebuilt the sample, examined TWFE, IV and matching. 12 of 12 checked numbers match. The main estimate falls to −0.0027 in fair comparisons.

Report code reproduction log 12-number check

Task 2

Sensitivity to unobserved confounding

Cinelli–Hazlett analysis. A confounder explaining 2.2% of leftover variation removes the effect.

Report code log robustness values

Task 3

Staggered difference-in-differences

Callaway–Sant'Anna, Borusyak–Jaravel–Spiess and Sun–Abraham. No harm in the main sample: +0.02 to +0.08, not significant.

Report BJS log Sun–Abraham log event-study viewer

13 November

Effort, missing months, solar area, weak instrument

Stricter policy goes with less observation. The Area effect vanishes holding effort fixed. The IV fails on exclusion, not strength.

Report effort log missingness log Anderson–Rubin log

19 November

Residualized sensitivity, dCDH estimators, data checks

dCDH gives −0.03 to −0.05 per point, weaker with controls; the effort pre-trend test fails.

Context

The paper was published on 20 August 2026. On 1 October 2026 Science posted an Editor's Note saying eLetters had raised technical concerns and that the editors were evaluating them (doi:10.1126/science.aem4102). The authors' data and code are public: their replication package.

This is an independent replication and evaluation. It is not affiliated with the paper's authors or the journal.

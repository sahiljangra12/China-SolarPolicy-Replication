Notes: This table tests whether the shift component of the instrument, the city-level climate policy uncertainty index (CPU), is correlated with observable ecological and socioeconomic characteristics. The dependent variable is city CPU, sourced from Ma et al.(66). Column (1) reports a cross-sectional regression of city-level mean CPU on city-level mean covariates. Column (2) reports a panel
regression controlling for city and year fixed effects with robust standard errors clustered by city. The key result is Column (2): after absorbing city and year fixed effects (consistent with the main IV specification), none of the covariates significantly predict the within-city time variation in CPU. Control variables include Temp (annual average temperature), Wind (annual average wind speed),

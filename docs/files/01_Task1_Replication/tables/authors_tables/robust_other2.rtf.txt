thresholds, respectively. The dependent variable is ShannonBD (the negative sum of the proportion of each bird species multiplied by its natural logarithm). ratio of total population to regional area), Duration (the logarithm of the ratio of birdwatching duration to the number of birdwatchers), Carbon (the logarithm of the carbon emissions), Water (the area of water divided by the total area), Green (the sum of the area of forests and shrubs divided by the total area), Farm (the area of farmland divided by the total area), Grass (the area of the

    C. Relax the instrument's key assumption
    
  
    Why this is here. The IV estimate is valid only if the instrument affects birds through PSI alone. Where PSI is fixed at 0, the instrument still moves Shannon. This shows the IV estimate for any assumed direct effect (Conley, Hansen & Rossi 2012).
